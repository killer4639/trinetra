title: One hundred and eight
date: 2026-09-20
summary: Notes on the rudraksha mala — a loop of beads, a loop of breath, and a loop of code.
---
A traditional mala has **108** beads and one more: the *sumeru*, or guru bead, where the
count begins and ends.

The mala on the front page is draggable. Pull a bead and the whole string follows —
the beads are placed by *arc length* along the string, so they keep their spacing as
they travel around the neck and disappear behind it.

| Beads | Tradition                   |
|------:|-----------------------------|
| 108   | full mala                   |
| 54    | half mala                   |
| 27    | quarter (one per nakshatra) |

This prototype wears the quarter mala, so every bead is large enough to touch.
