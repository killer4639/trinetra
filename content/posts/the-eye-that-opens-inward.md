title: The eye that opens inward
date: 2026-09-24
summary: Why this blog is built around a closed eye, and what it means to scroll your way into one.
---
Most websites begin with a door. This one begins with an eyelid.

Shiva is called **Trinetra** — the three-eyed. Two eyes look out at the world of forms.
The third, set vertically on the forehead, stays closed. When it opens, it does not
look *at* things; it looks *through* them.

Scrolling here is a small act of attention. Each notch of the wheel opens the eye a
little further, until there is nothing left to do but fall in.

> What you seek is seeking you.

Welcome inside.
